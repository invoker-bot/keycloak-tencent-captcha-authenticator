# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insulting comments, attacks on individuals or political views, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report project Code of Conduct incidents privately to the maintainers at [invoker-bot@outlook.com](mailto:invoker-bot@outlook.com). Include only the context needed to identify the incident and affected project space. Do not open a public issue or post the report in a discussion, pull request, or other public repository surface. Do not retaliate against a good-faith reporter.

Maintainers will acknowledge a conduct report within 5 business days and provide an initial status or request for necessary clarification within 10 business days. Reports and reporter identities will be handled as confidential information and shared only on a need-to-know basis with people required to investigate or enforce this policy. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when disclosure is required by law or necessary to address an immediate safety risk; the reporter will be informed before disclosure when legally and practically possible.

Suspected software vulnerabilities must instead use the repository's [private vulnerability reporting](SECURITY.md), not this conduct-report address. Do not include vulnerability details, credentials, proof fields, identifying user data, or client IP addresses in a conduct report.

Maintainers will evaluate context and may apply correction, warning, temporary exclusion, permanent exclusion, or other proportionate action. Enforcement decisions will be communicated privately to the reporter when possible, subject to the same confidentiality limits.
